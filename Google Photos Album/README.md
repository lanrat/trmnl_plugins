# [Google Photos Album](https://trmnl.com/recipes/321608?ref=mrlanrat)

Displays a random photo from a public [Google Photos](https://photos.google.com/) album on your TRMNL device.

## Description

Turns your TRMNL into an ever-changing photo frame, showing a different random photo from a shared album on each refresh. Photos are dithered for the e-ink display and never stretched.

## Settings

- **Public Album URL**: Share link to a public Google Photos album, set to "anyone with the link" (e.g. `https://photos.app.goo.gl/...`)
- **Display Mode**: `fill` (cover the screen, may crop 2 edges) or `contain` (show the whole photo with white bars on 2 edges)
